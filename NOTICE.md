## posit-dev/skills

As releases 2.0.0 e 2.1.0, e o histórico do Git a partir do commit `05fe9f5` (21/10/2026), incluem em `quarto-expert/references/` arquivos copiados ou adaptados do repositório [posit-dev/skills](https://github.com/posit-dev/skills): `callouts.md`, `citations.md`, `code-cells.md`, `conditional-content.md`, os seis `conversion-*.md`, `cross-references.md`, `diagrams.md`, `divs-and-spans.md`, `extensions.md`, `figures.md`, `layout.md`, `markdown-linting.md`, `shortcodes.md`, `tables.md`, `yaml-front-matter.md`, `alt-text.md` e `brand-yml.md`.
